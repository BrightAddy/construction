# Construction Company Website Architecture

## Home (Startseite)
*   **Hero Section:** High-resolution image or video of an active construction site, main headline, and a "Request a Quote" call-to-action (CTA).
*   **About Us Snapshot:** A brief paragraph introducing the company's experience and region of operation.
*   **Services Summary:** A 3-column grid highlighting core offerings (e.g., Civil Engineering, Residential Construction, Renovation) with links to learn more.
*   **Featured Projects:** A carousel displaying 3-4 flagship completed projects.
*   **Trust & Certifications:** Logos of industry certifications (e.g., TÜV, DIN EN ISO), safety standard badges, and trade association memberships.
*   **Footer:** Contact details, quick navigation links, and mandatory links to Impressum and Privacy Policy.

## Services (Leistungen)
*   **Page Header:** Title and introductory text detailing the company's technical capabilities.
*   **Service Details:** Individual sections for each specific service, outlining the scope of work, materials used, and expertise.
*   **Workflow/Process:** A 4-step infographic or timeline showing the project lifecycle (Consultation, Planning, Execution, Handover).
*   **Lead Generation CTA:** A section encouraging visitors to schedule a site visit or consultation.

## Projects (Referenzen)
*   **Filterable Gallery:** A grid of completed works with interactive filters (e.g., Commercial, Public Sector, Residential).
*   **Project Detail Views:** Clicking a project opens a section showing high-quality photos, project scope, timeline, and the specific solutions provided.
*   **Client Testimonials:** Quotes from previous clients, developers, or municipal partners.

## About Us (Über uns)
*   **Company History:** A chronological timeline showing the founding year and major company milestones.
*   **Mission & Values:** The company's commitment to sustainability, structural integrity, and worker safety.
*   **Leadership/Team:** Photos and brief professional bios of the managing directors and key engineers.
*   **Careers (Karriere):** Information on company culture, open roles, and apprenticeship (Ausbildung) opportunities.

## Contact (Kontakt)
*   **Direct Contact Info:** Phone number, email address, and official business hours.
*   **Contact Form:** Fields for Name, Email, Phone, Project Address, Project Description, and a mandatory GDPR consent checkbox.
*   **Location Map:** Embedded interactive map showing the headquarters or regional offices.

## Legal (Rechtliches - Mandatory)
*   **Impressum:** The legally required disclosure containing the registered company name, legal form (e.g., GmbH), managing directors, commercial register number (Handelsregister), and VAT ID.
*   **Datenschutzerklärung (Privacy Policy):** Detailed breakdown of data collection practices, third-party tools (like Analytics or Maps), and user data rights.
